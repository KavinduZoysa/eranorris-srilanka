Cinnamon Experience

🕰 3-hour half-day tour · or · One-night stay with full immersion

Sri Lanka produces the world's finest true cinnamon, and this immersive experience puts you at the heart of it. Guided by local artisans, you'll venture into a real cinnamon garden, learn to harvest and peel cinnamon bark by hand, and discover the age-old craft that has shaped Southern Sri Lankan life for generations. The one-night stay option extends into evening with a traditional home-cooked meal, a BBQ dinner, and optional turtle watching at the beach.

🐘 Wildlife Safari

Yala National Park Safari

🕰 Half-day guided tour · Pick-up & drop-off included

Yala National Park is one of Sri Lanka's most iconic wildlife destinations and has one of the highest leopard densities in the world. On this private safari, you'll explore the rugged scrublands and lagoons of Yala in an open-roofed jeep, with an experienced naturalist guide pointing out elephants, leopards, sloth bears, crocodiles, peacocks, and hundreds of bird species. We arrange comfortable pick-up from your hotel and handle all park entry details, so you can focus entirely on the wildlife.

What's Included

✔ Private safari jeep (open-top)
✔ Experienced naturalist guide
✔ Pick-up & drop-off from your hotel
✔ Yala National Park entry
✔ Elephants, leopards & diverse wildlife
✔ Birdwatching opportunities

🚣 Nature Activity

Kayaking in Rathgama Lagoon

🕰 Flexible duration · Available at sunrise & sunset · 50m from Birdsong Villa

Rathgama Lagoon is one of Southern Sri Lanka's hidden gems — a calm, glassy stretch of water bordered by mangroves, coconut palms, and quiet fishing villages. Paddle at your own pace and enjoy the stillness as cormorants skim the surface and colourful kingfishers dart through the trees. Whether you go at dawn as the mist lifts from the water, or at dusk as the sky turns gold and pink, kayaking here is a deeply peaceful, unforgettable experience.

What's Included

✔ Single or tandem kayak rental
✔ Life jackets & safety equipment
✔ Local guide (optional)
✔ Scenic mangrove routes
✔ Bird watching along the way
✔ Sunrise & sunset sessions available
